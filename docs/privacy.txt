Privacy Policy for ROUILLON ISABELLE

Effective date: May 12, 2026

ROUILLON ISABELLE is provided by ROUILLON ISABELLE. This policy explains how the Android app handles information.

Information handled by the app
The app lets you save shipment logs, container references, lead notes, visitor entries, follow-up dates, statuses, and local preferences. This information is stored on your device using local app storage.

No account or cloud sync
ROUILLON ISABELLE does not require account registration. It does not upload operation records to a server, provide cloud sync, or sell personal information.

Data collection
The app does not collect analytics, advertising identifiers, precise location, contacts, photos, microphone input, payment information, or health data.

Data sharing
The app does not share your operation records or preferences with ROUILLON ISABELLE or third parties.

Data retention and deletion
Your records remain on your device until you delete the app data or uninstall the app. Removing the app from your device removes locally stored app records according to Android system behavior.

Children
The app is a general productivity tool and is not directed to children under 13.

Security
Because operation records are stored locally, protect your device with the security features available on Android.
